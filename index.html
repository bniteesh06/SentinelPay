How SentinelPay decides what to flag

Two layers of detection working together, not one black box.

R

Rule-based checks

Foreign transactions, location mismatches, unusual hours, and velocity spikes are caught instantly using clear, explainable rules.

M

ML risk scoring

A trained model scores patterns rules alone would miss, learning from thousands of past transactions.

A

Alert dashboard

Flagged transactions land in one queue, sorted by risk, with the exact reason each one was flagged.

L

Audit history

Every decision, fraud or not fraud, is logged with who made the call and when.
